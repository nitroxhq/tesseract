### Core Architectural Decisions Highlighted

1. **Eliminating the Offline Stall (Fonts & Icons):**
   - In your current [index.html](file:///c:/Users/K1404887/Desktop/git-dev/tesseract/index.html#L37) and [styles.css](file:///c:/Users/K1404887/Desktop/git-dev/tesseract/styles.css#L2), fonts (`Inter`, `EB Garamond`, `Geist Mono`, `Instrument Sans`, `Instrument Serif`) and Phosphor duotone icons are fetched from Google Fonts and jsDelivr.

   - **Solution:** We replace external CDNs with local `@fontsource/*` packages and [`@phosphor-icons/react`](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/react).
